## 📥 Required Submission Components

To successfully complete your submission for **The Data & AI Challenge: Intelligent Candidate Discovery**, you must provide the following three items:

### 1. 💻 GitHub Repository Link
* **Requirement:** Provide the URL to your project's source code.
* **Access:** The repository **must** be set to public access.
* **Format Note:** The URL must explicitly include `http` or `https` (e.g., `https://github.com/sample_user`).

### 2. 📊 Presentation Pitch Deck (PDF Format)
* **Requirement:** Upload your presentation deck converted into a **PDF format**.
* **Content:** The deck must clearly outline your approach—what you built, why you built it that way, and how it works.
* **File Restrictions:** Supports PDF files up to **5 MB**.
* **Template Guidelines:** * It is **mandatory** to use the official submission template. 
    * Ensure your **Team ID** (provided during the intro session) and your **Team Name** are identical.
    * 📂 *Local Workspace Note:* A copy of this template is saved locally in your directory at:
      `Coding\Hackathons\India_runs_data_and_ai_challenge\resources`

### 3. 📈 Candidate Recommendation Output File (XLSX Format)
* **Requirement:** Upload the final spreadsheet containing the ranked output of your recommended candidates based on your model.
* **Format:** Excel / Spreadsheet (`.xlsx`).
* **File Restrictions:** Supports files up to **5 MB**.
